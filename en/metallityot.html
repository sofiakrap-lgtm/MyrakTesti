Myrak Oy — Services

Metalwork

Everything in metal

We restore metal balcony, terrace and other safety railings, as well as fences and gates, as part of facade works. We also supply roof safety products such as fire ladders and chimney sweep platforms.

Bespoke metalwork

Where required, we renew railings and other metal items entirely, with fabrication carried out either according to an existing design or based on the client's own plans.
